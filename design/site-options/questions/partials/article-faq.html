Common questions

What is the difference between AI Mode and AI Overviews?

An AI Overview is a summary Google shows on some results pages when its systems judge it useful. AI Mode is a separate experience that people open themselves, where they ask a question and keep asking follow-ups. Google’s guidance for site owners is the same for both.

Is Google AI Overview just Gemini?

No. Google says AI Overviews use a customised Gemini model that works alongside its existing Search systems, such as ranking and the Knowledge Graph. AI Mode uses a custom version of Gemini too, but both are features of Google Search and are separate from the Gemini app.

Do AI Mode and AI Overviews show the same sources?

Not necessarily. Google says the two may use different models and techniques, so the responses and links they show will vary, and that a different set of links will likely appear for the same query.

Do I need to optimise differently for AI Mode and AI Overviews?

No. Google says specific optimisation isn’t required for either, and the same foundational SEO practices apply. A page needs to be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet, and even then Google does not guarantee it will appear.

Can I turn off AI Mode or AI Overviews?

Searchers cannot switch AI Overviews off, but Google’s Web filter shows only text links. Site owners can use nosnippet, max-snippet, data-nosnippet or noindex to limit what both features use, though these also affect ordinary results. Blocking Google-Extended does not affect Search.
