Google AI Mode and SEO: what changes, and what to do about it

Google says the same SEO foundations apply to AI Mode, with no extra technical requirements. A page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet to be linked, and nobody can guarantee a citation.

What are Google AI Overviews?

AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries that Google shows on some searches, with links to supporting web pages. Google says no special markup is needed to be included, and no one can guarantee it.

How do you rank in Google AI Overviews?

Google says there is no special optimisation for AI Overviews. A page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet, and the usual helpful-content and technical SEO basics apply. Nobody can guarantee inclusion.

How do you track AI Overviews? What Google reports, what tools estimate, and what you can check yourself

Google counts AI Overview clicks inside your normal Search Console web data, with no separate report. Tracking means combining that, your analytics and sampled checks, and none of them gives a complete count.
